## 1. What CareCompanion is

CareCompanion is a demonstration of an eldercare-coordination assistant: medication reminders, daily check-ins and
alerts to family caregivers. It is a coordination and reminder tool. It is **not** a medical device, and nothing it says
is medical advice.

## 2. Not medical advice

- Medication interaction and allergy warnings come from a small, curated, informational table. They are not complete,
  may be out of date, and are no substitute for a pharmacist or doctor.
- CareCompanion does not diagnose, prescribe, or tell you how much of anything to take. Follow the instructions on your
  prescription and from your clinicians, not the add-on.
- Symptom escalation is a best-effort heuristic based on the words used. It can miss things and it can over-react.

## 3. Emergencies

If you or someone near you may be having a medical emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the United
States) immediately. Do this yourself, independently of anything CareCompanion says or does. CareCompanion cannot call
emergency services, and in the demo its "caregiver alerts" are only recorded and logged: no message reaches a real
person.

## 4. Demo data

The household in the add-on is fictional and its data is synthetic. Do not enter real health information about
yourself or anyone else. Data is held in memory only and is erased whenever the server restarts.
